My Shift Report

A real-time KPI and staffing platform that improves emergency-department nursing and patient care.

The problem

Every emergency department shift produces information that matters: who was working, what happened, which patients needed a callback, how the stroke and sepsis cases moved through their time-critical windows. Today most of that lives on paper, whiteboards, and memory. It fades at handoff, reaches leadership late or never, and is nearly impossible to learn from month over month.

What My Shift Report does

My Shift Report turns the charge nurse's end-of-shift report into living data. In a few minutes, the team records staffing for the shift, logs key events, tracks patient callbacks, and recognizes a standout colleague — and every entry becomes part of a record the whole department can see and search.

The same information is presented through three lenses, so each audience sees what it needs:

  • Frontline — the day-to-day view for a single department: today's shift report, callbacks, and reviews.
  • Leadership — the same picture across departments and hospitals, with analytics that turn daily entries into staffing and flow trends.
  • Quality — time-critical care in detail: stroke, heart attack, and sepsis cases followed step by step against their treatment-time goals, plus structured case review.

A set of standalone calculators rounds it out — tools that let any ED leader model staffing scenarios without creating an account.

Where it stands

The platform is live and in production use, with a companion demo site loaded with realistic sample data. You can explore every module yourself — sign-in takes only an email address.
