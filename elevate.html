Elevate

An AI-enhanced platform for emergency medicine resident development and residency-program management.

The problem

Residency programs generate an enormous amount of feedback — end-of-shift evaluations, surveys, interview notes — and most of it sits unread in inboxes and spreadsheets. Residents hear about their progress late and in fragments. Program leaders, who are responsible for turning interns into independent physicians in three short years, have no clear picture of who is thriving, who is drifting, and where coaching would matter most.

What Elevate does

Elevate gathers a residency's whole development loop into one place, organized around three questions: how residents learn, how they grow, and how the program understands them.

  • Learn — residents practice the hardest parts of the job on their own time: AI-guided difficult conversations and clinical cases with feedback.
  • Grow — a private voice journal; a resident talks through a shift and gets an automatic transcript and summary, building a record of their own development.
  • Understand — evaluations and surveys distilled into something leadership can act on: strengths-and-weaknesses profiles for each resident and a fifteen-point view of performance across emotional, professional, and clinical dimensions.

A companion family of tools — Progress Check, the Interview Assessment Tool, and Pulse Check — carries the same approach into residency evaluation surveys, applicant interviews, and provider evaluations.

Where it stands

Elevate is live and in production use at an emergency medicine residency program, where it has analyzed thousands of faculty evaluations across roughly fifty residents.
