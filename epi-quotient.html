EPI-Quotient

A developmental framework for optimizing the inflection points of professional growth.

The problem

Healthcare turnover and hiring mistakes are usually described as people problems. More often they are measurement problems. Most organizations cannot describe what “good” looks like beyond clinical competence — so feedback stays vague, recognition feels arbitrary, hiring leans on gut instinct, and the warning signs of a departure stay invisible until the resignation letter arrives.

What EPI Quotient is

EPI Quotient gives teams a shared language for performance. It scores each person across three dimensions — EQ, how someone is with people; PQ, how they own the work — standards, reliability, follow-through; and IQ, how good they are at the craft — through fifteen observable attributes. Together they form a performance fingerprint that can be followed over time.

Followed over the years, those fingerprints reveal the inflection points of a career: steady climbers, late bloomers, and peak-and-decline patterns that often precede a resignation by months. Because the same fifteen attributes describe reviews, team assessments, hiring profiles, and interviews, the framework runs as one continuous loop — what you learn about the team shapes who you hire, and how you coach.

The framework comes in editions for medical teams and for whole dental teams, and its first working module — structured evaluations called Pulse Check — is already in use inside the Elevate platform.

Where it stands

EPI Quotient is an early product in active development, with its first organizational pilot being set up now.
