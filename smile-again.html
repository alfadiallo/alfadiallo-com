Smile Again

A domestic and international nonprofit providing comprehensive dentistry to survivors of domestic violence.

Why it exists

A damaged smile is one of the most visible and lasting injuries a person can carry — and restorative dentistry is priced far beyond the reach of the people who need it most. For survivors of domestic violence, that damage is a daily reminder of what they survived. For underserved communities, comprehensive dental care simply never comes. Smile Again exists for both: restoring smiles as a way of rebuilding lives.

What Smile Again does

Smile Again is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that delivers free, comprehensive, high-standard dentistry through two arms. At home, volunteer dentists provide ongoing pro bono care for survivors rebuilding their lives. Internationally, the flagship program is an annual week-long dental brigade in Guaimaca, Honduras — a full clinic treating roughly four hundred patients in four days, with a same-trip digital lab on site: intraoral scanning, 3D printing, and ceramic crowns milled the same day a patient is seen.

Industry partners donate equipment, supplies, and lab technology, so more of every dollar reaches patients. The work to date:

  • 648 dental patients treated, plus 150 medical patients
  • 3,900+ procedures delivered, including 140+ same-day ceramic crowns
  • $633K in donated dental care

Where it stands

Active and growing — the most recent brigade ran in July 2026, and a new digital platform for the organization is in the works.
